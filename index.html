What we do

The everyday work of keeping a business, a farm or a household right with the tax authorities. All services

  • Annual accounts

    Year-end accounts for sole traders, partnerships and limited companies, prepared and filed for you.

  • Audit

    Statutory and voluntary audits, carried out by registered auditors.

  • Personal tax

    Tax returns in Northern Ireland and the Republic, including income that crosses the border.

  • Company tax

    Corporation tax returns and planning for companies in either jurisdiction.

  • VAT and payroll

    VAT returns, payroll and PAYE, filed on time so you don't have to think about them.

  • Bookkeeping

    Records kept in order through the year, so the year end holds no surprises.

  • Company secretarial

    Setting up a company and keeping its statutory filings up to date.

  • Financial advice

    Financial advice that sits alongside your accounts and tax.
